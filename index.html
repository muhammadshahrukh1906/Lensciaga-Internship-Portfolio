Process
03 / Content strategy

Creative
Process

A practical process I used to turn product features into clear, consistent and engaging content that supports Lensciaga's identity and commercial goals.

01

Research

Reviewed the product, competing eyewear content, and audience preferences to identify the strongest angle for each post.

02

Positioning

Selected the most relevant benefit, mood and visual hook so each frame had a clear reason to stand out.

03

Creative Direction

Matched colour, composition, typography and tone to the frame while keeping every piece recognisably Lensciaga.

04

Production

Planned images, reels, carousels and interactive elements around the format best suited to the product story.

05

Optimisation

Reviewed pacing, hierarchy, mobile readability and calls to action before finalising each piece for publication.
